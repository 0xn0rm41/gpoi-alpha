**Processor:** [Operator legal name], [address], [registration or tax number], represented by [name, role]. Privacy contact: [email].

Together "the Parties". This agreement (the "DPA") forms part of the service agreement dated [date] (the "Service Agreement") for the provision of the Hub Studenti platform to the Controller's school community.

## 1. Subject matter, duration, nature and purpose (art. 28.3)

1.1 The Processor hosts and operates the Hub Studenti web application for the Controller's community (identified in the system as the school "[school name]"), so that the Controller's students can share ideas, polls, notes, book listings and event information.
1.2 Duration: as long as the Service Agreement is in force, plus the return/deletion period in clause 10.
1.3 Nature of processing: storage, hosting, transmission by email of service messages, backup, deletion; no profiling and no automated decisions.
1.4 Purpose: providing the service to the Controller, on the Controller's documented instructions.

## 2. Types of data and data subjects

| | |
|---|---|
| Data subjects | Students and staff members of the Controller who register in its community, including **minors** |
| Personal data | First name, surname, email, optional class, password hash, consent record (date and notice version), last access date, content created by users (texts, photos, votes, participations, book requests, reports), session and token hashes |
| Special categories | None requested. Users are told not to enter any; if some appear in content the Processor removes them on request |

## 3. Documented instructions (art. 28.3.a)

3.1 The Processor processes the data only on the Controller's documented instructions, which are this DPA, the Service Agreement and the Controller's written instructions given through the contacts named above, including to transfers to a third country (none permitted: clause 8).
3.2 If the Processor believes an instruction infringes data protection law, it informs the Controller immediately.
3.3 The Processor does not use the data for its own purposes. In particular: **no advertising, no third-party trackers, no sale or sharing of data, no use of the data for profiling or for training of models**.

## 4. Confidentiality (art. 28.3.b)

Persons authorised to process the data (the Processor's staff and the Controller's "admins", see clause 11) are bound by confidentiality, by contract or by statutory duty, and receive written instructions.

## 5. Security (art. 28.3.c, art. 32)

The Processor applies the measures in **Annex II**, appropriate to the risk, considering the data subjects are minors.

## 6. Sub-processors (art. 28.2 and 28.4)

6.1 The Controller gives a **general written authorisation** for the sub-processors listed in **Annex III** [or: specific authorisation, delete as appropriate].
6.2 The Processor informs the Controller of intended additions or replacements at least [30] days in advance by email; the Controller may object on reasonable data protection grounds within [15] days. If the Parties cannot agree, the Controller may terminate the affected service.
6.3 The Processor imposes on each sub-processor the same data protection obligations as in this DPA and remains fully liable to the Controller for them.

## 7. Assistance with data subject rights (art. 28.3.e)

7.1 The platform lets users access and export their data, correct them and delete their account themselves. The Processor assists the Controller with the remaining requests (restriction, objection, requests that a user cannot make alone) within [5] working days of the Controller's request, following [PROCEDURES.md](PROCEDURES.md).
7.2 If a data subject contacts the Processor directly, the Processor forwards the request to the Controller without undue delay and does not reply on the merits unless instructed.

## 8. International transfers (chapter V)

The Processor and its sub-processors store and process the data **in the EU/EEA only**. No transfer to a third country or international organisation takes place without the Controller's prior written consent and a valid transfer mechanism (adequacy decision, art. 45, or standard contractual clauses, art. 46, with a transfer impact assessment).

## 9. Breach notification and assistance (art. 28.3.f, arts. 33-34)

9.1 The Processor notifies the Controller of a personal data breach **without undue delay and in any case within [24 / 48] hours** of becoming aware of it, with the information available (nature, categories and approximate numbers of data subjects and records, likely consequences, measures taken), and keeps the Controller updated.
9.2 The Processor helps the Controller with breach notification to the authority and communication to data subjects, with the DPIA (art. 35) and with prior consultation (art. 36). The Controller decides whether and how to notify.

## 10. Deletion or return (art. 28.3.g)

At the end of the services, at the Controller's choice, the Processor returns the data (machine-readable export) and/or deletes all the Controller's community data, including photos, within [30] days; copies in backups are overwritten within **30 days** of deletion. The Processor confirms deletion in writing on request. Retention in the meantime follows the product settings: unconfirmed accounts 3 days, inactive accounts 12 months, resolved reports 90 days, backups 30 days (or as the Controller instructs in writing, within what the product can configure).

## 11. School admins (persons authorised by the Controller)

11.1 The Controller designates the student representatives or staff who receive the "admin" role in its community: [names / process]. The Processor activates the role on the Controller's written request.
11.2 Admins can delete content in their community, read reports, see the member count, manage the invite code and the optional email-domain restriction, and publish events and announcements. They cannot see members' emails. The Controller instructs them in writing and revokes the role when they leave.
11.3 The Controller is responsible for moderation decisions and for escalating safeguarding matters inside the school; the Processor supports with its moderation tools.

## 12. Audit and information (art. 28.3.h)

The Processor provides the information needed to demonstrate compliance and allows and contributes to audits, including inspections, by the Controller or an auditor it appoints: [once a year / on reasonable notice of 15 working days], during business hours, under confidentiality, avoiding disruption and without access to other controllers' data. The open-source code can also be inspected at the source link published by the service.

## 13. Processor's own obligations

The Processor keeps a record of processing as processor (art. 30.2), cooperates with the supervisory authority (art. 31) and appoints a DPO if required (art. 37). It informs the Controller of changes that affect compliance.

## 14. Liability, term, law

Liability follows the Service Agreement and arts. 82-83 GDPR [lawyer to complete]. This DPA lasts as long as the Processor processes data for the Controller. Governing law and jurisdiction: [law of ..., courts of ...]. In case of conflict on data protection matters, this DPA prevails.

## Annex II: Technical and organisational measures (art. 32)

- Passwords hashed with scrypt and random salt; session and email tokens stored only as SHA-256 hashes; session cookies `HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Lax`, `Secure` over HTTPS.
- CSRF protection; rate limits on login and registration; email verification; no disclosure of whether an email exists.
- Data separation between communities: every content query is scoped to the user's school; photos served only to logged-in members of the same school.
- Server-side permission checks; admin role assignable only by the Processor from the command line; admins cannot see emails.
- Input validation, parameterised SQL, image content checks, strict CSP, no third-party resources, no analytics.
- HTTPS everywhere; OS and runtime updates; firewall; server access limited to authorised staff with strong authentication [2FA]; protected configuration file.
- **Encrypted** off-site backups, kept 30 days; restore tests [frequency]; erasures re-applied after a restore.
- Breach procedure and moderation procedure ([PROCEDURES.md](PROCEDURES.md)); staff confidentiality commitments and training.
- Automated tests; open-source code subject to public review and a vulnerability policy ([../cra/VULNERABILITY-POLICY.md](../cra/VULNERABILITY-POLICY.md)).
