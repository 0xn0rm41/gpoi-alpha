# Feasibility study - Hub Studenti

Tools to decide, with evidence, whether and how Hub Studenti moves from a school test to its own domain, host and (optionally) a revenue model.

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [requirements.md](requirements.md) | Requirements gathering: stakeholders, elicitation, user stories, MoSCoW, acceptance criteria, traceability matrix (with an applied example) |
| [template_fattibilita.md](template_fattibilita.md) | Feasibility study template: technical, legal/regulatory, economic, operational, schedule, risks, go/no-go |
| [ROI_Payback_VAL_TIR.md](ROI_Payback_VAL_TIR.md) | ROI, payback, discounted payback, NPV (VAL/VAN), IRR (TIR), break-even: formulas, Italian terms, one worked **hypothetical** example |
| [template_gantt.xlsx](template_gantt.xlsx) | Project plan with automatic Gantt bars and today marker (open in Excel or LibreOffice) |

Companion folder: [../analisi_mercato/README.md](../analisi_mercato/README.md) (market evidence feeds the economic section here).

## Suggested order

1. Gather requirements ([requirements.md](requirements.md)) - what must the product and the service do?
2. Collect market evidence ([../analisi_mercato/FRAMEWORKS.md](../analisi_mercato/FRAMEWORKS.md)).
3. Fill the feasibility template; compute the economics with the ROI document.
4. Plan the launch in the Gantt sheet.
5. Take a go/no-go decision and record it with date and participants.

## Notes

- All numbers in examples are **hypothetical placeholders**. Replace them with your own data before drawing conclusions.
- Legal sections are checklists for discussion with a qualified professional; they are **not legal advice**.
- Context: students at an Italian high school; after the test ends the platform is expected to move to its own domain/host. Licence plan: AGPL-3.0-or-later with a commercial/dual license and hosted SaaS. Note that the repository `LICENSE` file currently states MIT (see [../../README.md](../../README.md)); changing the licence is a decision to take deliberately (see the legal section of the template).
