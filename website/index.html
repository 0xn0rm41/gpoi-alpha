One place for everything that happens at school.

Events, class calendar, shared notes, polls, class-assembly requests and vending-machine availability — built by students, for students. Free and open source (AGPL-3.0).

Open the source codePropose it to your school

What it does

Calendar

Month, week, day and agenda views like Google Calendar. Tests and homework by class; import .ics, export, or subscribe from your phone calendar.

Vending machines

What is inside, how much it costs, and whether it has run out — reported by students.

Class assemblies

Request an assembly; the school sees the rules (notice, hours, per month) and answers in the app.

Bar pre-order

For schools with a bar (hotel and catering schools). Pay at pickup. Enabled only on the school's e-mail request.

Follow bell

Follow ideas, polls and sections. Get the news in the bell, and by e-mail only if you choose.

Italian and English

The interface, e-mails and notifications in both languages.

Ideas, polls, notes, books

Propose ideas, vote, share notes with photos, give books a second life.

Works on phones

Mobile-first layout, installable from the browser, no app store.

For schools

A school gets its own community with an invite code. Student representatives or staff manage content; calendar, vending machines and assemblies are on by default; the bar module is optional and switched on by us after the school writes to us. Data is stored in the EU. We never ask for register (Classeviva) passwords.

Privacy first

Minimal data, 14+ only, one-click export and deletion of your account, no ads, no trackers, no third-party requests from the pages. Our legal documents are drafts and will be reviewed with a data-protection professional and each school's DPO before launch.

Status

Working prototype with automated tests. Not yet used by real schools; pilot planned. Project name, domain and hosting are still being decided.
